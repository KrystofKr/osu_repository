### Otázka (Front)

Describe security safeguards.

### Odpověď (Back)

Security safeguards are used to implement security policy. We discern three types of safeguards:

- **administrative** - internal policies and process management (security awareness training);

- **physical** - physical mechanisms for the detection and prevention of security events and incidents (door lock, fence);

- **logical **- logical mechanisms for the detection and prevention of security events and incidents (firewall, anti-malware).

### Doplňující pole (Extra)

According to VoKB both physical and logical safeguards fall under the same category called technical safeguards (technická opatření).

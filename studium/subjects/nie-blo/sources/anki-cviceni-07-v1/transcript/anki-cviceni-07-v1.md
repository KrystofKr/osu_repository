Fuzz testing (fuzzing) is an automated testing technique that involves providing invalid, unexpected, or random data as inputs to a computer program and monitoring it for exceptions. Fuzz testing can use the following approaches that differ in the degree of knowledge of the internal structure of the tested system:

- **black box testing** - no knowledge,

- **white box testing** - complete knowledge,

- **gray box testing** - partial or otherwise limited knowledge.

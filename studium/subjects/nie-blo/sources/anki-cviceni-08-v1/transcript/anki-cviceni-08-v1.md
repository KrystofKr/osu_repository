### Otázka (Front)

Describe the sctructure of a security audit of a smart contract.

### Odpověď (Back)

Security audit of a smart contract should contain the following:

- **system overview** - structure of the smart contract, interactions between its components;

- **trust model** - subjects that may interact with the smart contract and their permissions, trust assumptions;

- **methodology** - approach and techniques used during the audit;

- **tool-based analysis** - automated tools such as static analysers;

- **local deployment** - deployment on a testnet, manual search for vulnerabilities;

- **code review** - manual search for vulnerabilities, verification of adherence to best practices;

- **fuzz tests** - ensurance of correct functionality and resilience to random or unexpected inputs;
- **list of findings** - discovered findings and remediation recommendations;

- **executive summary** - key findings summarized for top-level executives.

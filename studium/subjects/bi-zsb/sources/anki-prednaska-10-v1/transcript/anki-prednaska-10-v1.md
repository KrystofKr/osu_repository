### Otázka (Front)

Describe memorandum of understanding (MoU) and its use in business continuity management (BCM).

### Odpověď (Back)

Memorandum of understanding (MoU) also known as memorandum of agreement (MoA) is a formal agreement between two or more parties that outlines a common line of action. MoU is commonly used in business continuity management (BCM) to ensure that if one of the parties experiences a disaster or other disruptive event, the other parties will share their resources in order to sustain organizational operations of the affected party.

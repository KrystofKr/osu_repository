### Otázka (Front)

What is the difference between host-based and network-based vulnerability assesment?

### Odpověď (Back)

**Host-based vulnerability assesment** is usually implemented using the manager/agent architecture, where the agent is directly installed on the assessed system and reports the system's conformance to the manager from the perspective of an authenticated user. In contrast, **network-based vulnerability assesment** usually takes the form of an external network scan from the perspective of an unauthenticated user.

### Otázka (Front)

Describe the process maturity model.

### Odpověď (Back)

The process maturity model categorizes processes (such as patch management) into the following categories based on their formality, optimization, and efficiency:

- **initial** - the process is undocumented,

- **repeatable** - the process is documented to the degree that allows repeatability,

- **defined** - the process is defined as a standard business process,

- **capable** - the process is managed in accordance with agreed-upon metrics,

- **efficient** - the process includes deliberate optimization and improvement.

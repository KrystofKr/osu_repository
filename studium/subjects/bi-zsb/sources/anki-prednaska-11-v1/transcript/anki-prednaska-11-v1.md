Describe disaster recovery plan (DRP). Describe the phases of its execution.

### Odpověď (Back)

Disaster recovery plan (DRP) is a plan that contains instructions on how to respond to disasters and other disruptive events and restore normal organizational operations. The execution of a disaster recovery plan usually follows these phases:

- assess,

- recover,

- restore normal operations,

- review.

Definujte maximum, minimum, infimum a supremum pro funkce.

### Odpověď (Back)

Pro \(f: D_f \to \mathbb{ R }\) a množinu \(M \subset D_f\) klademe
\(\inf \limits_M f = \inf \limits_{ x \in M } f(x) := \inf \{ f(x) \mid x \in M \}\),
\(\sup \limits_M f = \sup \limits_{ x \in M } f(x) := \sup\{ f(x) \mid x \in M \}\).
Buď \(f: D_f \to \mathbb{ R }\) spojitá funkce a \(M \subset D_f\) uzavřený omezený interval. Potom klademe
\(\max \limits_M f = \max \limits_{ x \in M } f(x) := \max \{ f(x) \mid x \in M \}\),
\(\min \limits_M f = \min \limits_{ x \in M } f(x) := \min \{ f(x) \mid x \in M \}\).

### Otázka (Front)

What is a controller area network (CAN) bus?

### Odpověď (Back)

Controller area network (CAN) bus is a vehicle bus that allows the systems of a vehicle to communicate using a message-based protocol that runs on the physical and link layers. All messages sent on the CAN bus are broadcasted and therefore received by all of the connected systems, including the sender. The messages are differentiated using identifiers with lower identifiers having higher transmission priority.

### Otázka (Front)

What are Unified Diagnostic Services (UDS)?

### Odpověď (Back)

Unified Diagnostic Services (UDS) is a session and application layer protocol based on the request/response architecture. UDS runs on top of the controller area network (CAN) bus protocol and is commonly used in automotive to configure and diagnose vehicle electronic control units (ECU).
